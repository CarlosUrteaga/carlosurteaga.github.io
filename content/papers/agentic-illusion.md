## Overview

The Agentic Illusion describes a gap between visible functional achievement and operational preparedness in AI-assisted software development. A working system can demonstrate useful capabilities before its architecture, validation, governance, and compliance evidence are ready for sustained operation.

The framework identifies four manifestations: architecture drift, validation lag, governance gaps, and compliance debt. It draws on two empirical cases and proposes candidate indicators for further assessment, rather than treating those indicators as established measures.
